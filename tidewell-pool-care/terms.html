1. About these terms

These terms illustrate how a residential pool-care company might present its service agreement on a website. Because Tidewell Pool Care is a demonstration, no contract is formed, no booking is real, and no payment can be taken through this site.

2. Sample services and pricing

All services and "from" prices shown on this site (for example, weekly maintenance, equipment repair, green-to-clean, openings and closings, and filter and pump service) are illustrative figures for the demo. They are not offers and do not reflect any real company's rates. A live business would confirm scope and price with a written, on-site quote.

3. Pool-service disclaimer

In a real engagement, pool maintenance and repair involve water chemistry, electrical equipment, and safety considerations. A genuine provider would describe access requirements and the homeowner's responsibility to maintain safe pool barriers and fencing.

4. Refunds & satisfaction

Because this is a concept demo, no money ever changes hands and there is nothing to refund. The text below is sample language showing how a real pool-care business might present a refund and satisfaction policy:

  • Service guarantee. If a completed visit does not meet the agreed scope, the customer notifies the provider within 48 hours and the provider re-services the issue at no additional charge.
  • Prepaid plans. Unused weeks on a prepaid maintenance plan are refundable on a pro-rated basis, less any services already performed, when a customer cancels with reasonable notice.
  • Repairs & parts. Labor is typically non-refundable once performed; parts may be subject to the manufacturer's warranty and any restocking terms.
  • Quotes. On-site quotes are free and carry no obligation.

A live site would state exact timeframes, eligibility, and how to request a refund, along with a real business contact.
