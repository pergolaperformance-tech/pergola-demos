Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

This is a concept demo. Tidewell Pool Care is a fictional sample business created by Pergola Performance to demonstrate website design. It is not a real company, does not provide real services, and is not affiliated with any real business; any resemblance is coincidental. This policy is sample text.

1. Overview

This page describes, in plain language, how a privacy policy might read for a residential pool-care business. Because Tidewell Pool Care is a demonstration, no real customer data is collected, processed, sold, or stored through this site.

2. Information this demo does not collect

The contact form on this site is a demonstration only. When you submit it, the information is validated in your browser to show a sample success state and is then discarded. It is not transmitted to a server, emailed to anyone, or saved.

  • No names, phone numbers, email addresses, or messages are stored.
  • No accounts, logins, or payment details are handled here.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set by this demo.

3. What a live version would describe

A real pool-service business would typically explain what contact and property information it collects to schedule service and provide quotes, how long it keeps that information, the third parties (such as scheduling or payment processors) it shares it with, and the choices customers have. A live policy would also list a real business contact for privacy requests.

4. Analytics and hosting

This demo may be served by a standard static-site host that records basic, aggregated request logs (such as page views and approximate region) for security and performance. These logs are not used to identify individual visitors for this demonstration.

5. Children's privacy

This demo is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

6. Changes

Because this is sample content, it may change at any time without notice as the demonstration is updated.

7. Contact

Questions about this demonstration can be directed to its creator, Pergola Performance, at pergolaperformance@gmail.com. The Tidewell brand, phone number, and email shown elsewhere on the site are fictional placeholders.
