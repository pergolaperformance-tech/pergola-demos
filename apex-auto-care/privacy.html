Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Apex Auto Care ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it when you contact us or use this website.

What we collect

  • Booking details — your name, phone, vehicle, and the service you describe when you request a callback.
  • Basic usage data — standard technical information to keep the site working.

We don't take card payments on this website; payment happens in person at the shop.

How we use it

  • To call you back and schedule your service.
  • To provide an estimate and complete the work you request.
  • To follow up about your vehicle when needed.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. We only share it with service providers needed to run our business (such as our website host), and only as required to operate.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information any time — just call (555) 318-2240.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
