Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Cedarline Construction Co. is a fictional concept demo by Pergola Performance — not a real business. The policy below illustrates what a real general contractor's privacy page would cover.

Information we collect

When you submit the quote form, we collect the details you choose to provide: your name, phone number, email address, project type, and any project details you write in the message field.

How we use it

  • To respond to your quote request and discuss your project.
  • To prepare estimates and schedule site visits.
  • To send project-related updates you've asked for.

How we share it

We share your information only with the service providers needed to operate the business — for example, the form-handling service that delivers your message to our inbox. We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & analytics

A live version of this site may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how many people visit. This concept demo does not load advertising trackers.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the contact details you've shared by emailing hello@cedarline-construction.example.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@cedarline-construction.example or call (713) 555-0148.
