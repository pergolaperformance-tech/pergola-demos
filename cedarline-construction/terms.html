Terms of Service

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Cedarline Construction Co. is a fictional concept demo by Pergola Performance — not a real business. The terms below illustrate what a real general contractor's terms page would cover.

Using this website

This website provides information about our services and a form to request a quote. Submitting the form starts a conversation — it does not create a binding agreement to perform work.

Quotes & estimates

Estimates are based on the information you provide and a site visit where needed. The work itself is governed by a separate written contract that defines scope, schedule, price, and payment terms before any work begins.

Scheduling & communication

We aim to follow up on quote requests promptly. Timelines shared during early conversations are planning estimates and are confirmed in your written contract.

Refund & deposit policy

For a real general contractor, deposits and refunds would be handled like this:

  • Deposits secure your spot on the schedule and cover initial materials and design work.
  • Before work begins: if you cancel before any materials are ordered or work has started, your deposit is refundable, less any design or permitting fees already incurred.
  • After work begins: refunds are calculated against work completed and materials purchased, per the milestone schedule in your signed contract.
  • How to request: email hello@cedarline-construction.example and we'll walk through your specific situation.
