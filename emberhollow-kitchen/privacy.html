Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Concept notice: Emberhollow is a fictional restaurant built as a demonstration by Pergola Performance. It is not a real business and is not an actual client. This page is sample legal copy that shows how a real venue's privacy policy would read. No data is genuinely collected, stored, or processed here.

1. Overview

This policy explains how a restaurant like Emberhollow would handle information collected through its website. Because this is a demonstration, the reservation form does not transmit or save any data — submissions are handled entirely in your browser and discarded.

2. Information we would collect

On a live version of this site, the reservation request form would collect only what you provide:

  • Your name
  • A phone number and/or email address
  • The details of your requested visit (date, time, party size, and any notes)

We would not request sensitive personal information and would never ask for payment details through this form.

3. How it would be used

Reservation details would be used solely to contact you, confirm your booking, and prepare for your visit (for example, noting an allergy or a celebration). It would not be sold or shared for advertising.

4. Cookies & analytics

This demonstration site uses no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. A live restaurant site might use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand traffic; if so, that use would be disclosed here.

5. Data retention

A live venue would keep reservation details only as long as needed to serve you and meet ordinary record-keeping needs, then delete them. In this demo, nothing is retained.

6. Your choices

On a live site you would be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any information you submitted, by contacting the restaurant directly.

7. Contact

Questions about this demonstration can be directed to Pergola Performance at pergolaperformance@gmail.com. See also our Terms.
