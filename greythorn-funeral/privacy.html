Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

This is sample privacy copy for a concept demonstration site. In a live deployment for a real funeral home, this policy would describe how that business handles your information. It is written here only to show the kind of content a finished site would include.

What we would collect

  • Contact details — the name, phone number, email, and message you choose to share through the contact form when reaching out for help.
  • Basic usage data — standard technical information a website needs to load and function correctly.

On this demo, the contact form does not actually send or store anything — submissions are handled entirely in your browser to show a success state.

How it would be used

  • To respond to your message and arrange a time to speak.
  • To provide the services you ask about.

We would never use a grieving family's details for marketing without clear, separate consent.

Sharing

A real Greythorn would not sell your information. It would be shared only with the providers needed to operate the business (such as a website host), and only to the extent required.

Sensitive circumstances

Information shared at a difficult time deserves extra care. A live site would limit access to such details to the staff directly helping your family.

Your choices

You could ask to review, update, or delete information about you at any time by calling (555) 019-9074.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
