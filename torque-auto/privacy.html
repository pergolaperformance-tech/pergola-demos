Privacy Policy

Last updated · June 2026

Concept demo notice: TORQUE Auto Studio is a fictional shop built as a demonstration by Pergola Performance. It is not a real business and has no real customers. The form on the site does not send, transmit or store any data — it is illustrative only. The policy below is written as a realistic example of what a working studio site would publish.

1. What we would collect

If this were a live studio, the booking and quote forms would collect only what is needed to respond to you:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • Vehicle details and the service you are interested in.
  • Any message you choose to send us.

We would not ask for payment details through the website, and we would never sell or rent your information.

2. How it would be used

Contact details would be used solely to reply to your enquiry, confirm an appointment, and provide a quote. We would not add you to marketing lists without your clear consent.

3. Cookies & analytics

This demo sets no tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics. A production site would disclose any analytics or cookies used and offer a way to opt out.

4. Data retention

In a live setting, enquiry details would be kept only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal record-keeping requirements, then deleted.

5. Your rights

You would have the right to ask what data is held about you, to correct it, and to request its deletion. Requests would be handled within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Contact

Because this is a concept demo, there is no live contact channel. Questions about the demo itself can be directed to the agency, Pergola Performance.
